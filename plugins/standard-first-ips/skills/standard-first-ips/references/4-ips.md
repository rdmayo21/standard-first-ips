# Phase 4: write, review, sign

## Draft

Use `ips-template.md`.
- Put the standard at the top, verbatim.
- Write each rule so someone else could execute it without asking: exact bands, order of
  operations on an evaluation day, where new money goes, what happens at the goal date.
- Put the reason for each rule beside it, with the evidence and what would re-open it.
- Keep a changelog.

## Blind consistency review

Give a critic both the previous signed version and the draft, plus the decisions, and ask four
questions:
1. Does the draft enact exactly the decisions, and nothing more? Unauthorized additions are the
   most common defect. In the source run, the drafts gained a yield pre-check, a mandatory
   overflow rule and a general exception to the tie rule. Nobody had asked for any of them.
2. Are there leftovers of retired rules anywhere?
3. Is every state covered unambiguously? For example: each sleeve above or below its band,
   contributions, withdrawals, the quit month.
4. Do the numbers quoted match the evidence files?

Fix, then run a short confirm pass. Repeat until a pass finds nothing material. Two to four
passes is normal.

## Sign

- Give the person a readable copy (a PDF formatted for their phone helps).
- Summarize what changed in five lines or fewer.
- Ask for an explicit signature reply: "sign v2".
- Record the signature verbatim, and mark the old version superseded.

No trade follows automatically. If the new policy implies trades, list them separately for the
person to execute.
